Beyond Artificial Intelligence

Technology has always extended human capability. Rather than creating algorithmic systems destined to function in complete isolation, Project ASCEND imagines a future where biological frameworks are directly synchronized with cloud-scale machine processing modules.

We do not seek to build autonomous replacement machines. We design augmented biological structures that optimize and expand organic human thought metrics.
